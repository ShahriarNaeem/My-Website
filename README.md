## About This Website

Welcome to my personal academic and professional portfolio.

This website documents my academic background, professional experience, research publications, technical projects, and evolving research interests at the intersection of **civil engineering, environmental sustainability, geospatial data science, artificial intelligence, and sustainable finance**.

With a foundation in civil engineering and infrastructure project management, I am interested in understanding how engineering systems, environmental evidence, and data-driven approaches can contribute to climate resilience, sustainable infrastructure investment, and evidence-based policy.

The website serves as a central platform for connecting with academics, researchers, potential graduate supervisors, professional collaborators, and organizations working toward sustainable development.

## Research Interests

My interdisciplinary interests focus on four connected areas:

- **Climate and Sustainable Finance** — Climate-risk assessment, sustainable investment, ESG analysis, climate finance, and resilient infrastructure investment.
- **Environmental Data Science** — Air quality assessment, atmospheric pollution, environmental monitoring, statistical analysis, and data-driven environmental research.
- **Geospatial Intelligence and Remote Sensing** — GIS, satellite-derived environmental indicators, spatial analysis, and climate-related risk assessment.
- **Sustainable Infrastructure and Urban Systems** — Infrastructure economics, transport systems, environmental impact assessment, urban resilience, and sustainable development.

I am particularly interested in connecting environmental and geospatial evidence with financial and analytical frameworks to support more informed decisions about climate resilience and sustainable investment.

## Featured Research

### 1. Air Quality and the COVID-19 Lockdown in Dhaka

Research examining changes in air pollution during the COVID-19-induced nationwide lockdown in Dhaka, Bangladesh, using environmental observations and statistical analysis.

**Focus areas:** PM2.5, PM10, NO₂, O₃, air quality, and urban environmental sustainability.

### 2. Environmental and Traffic Impacts of MRT Line-6

Research exploring the environmental and socioeconomic implications of metro rail construction, including traffic disruption, air pollution concerns, and urban infrastructure development.

**Focus areas:** Sustainable transport, environmental assessment, traffic systems, and urban planning.

### 3. Geospatial Environmental Analysis

Exploration of GIS, remote sensing, and computational methods for examining environmental patterns and supporting spatially informed decision-making.

**Focus areas:** Spatial data, satellite observations, environmental indicators, and Python-based analysis.

### 4. Machine Learning for Transport and Environmental Applications

Application-oriented work exploring statistical learning and machine learning techniques for analyzing transport-related data and identifying patterns relevant to decision-making.

**Focus areas:** Python, scikit-learn, Random Forest, XGBoost, and predictive analytics, where applicable to individual projects.
